On the side I ship my own products: 10 Sentences, a German learning platform with 100+ users; Batcheet, a 30K+ node trust graph for referral hiring; LinkInsertion.ai, an agentic outreach tool with paying customers; and SyncMyTime, a time and weather database for remote teams.

Master Thesis — Group Co-Speech Gesture Synthesis
MPI Informatics  |  Saarbrücken  ·  MS Computer Science, Saarland University
  • Built a speech- and text-conditioned 3D gesture generation model with latent diffusion: a Transformer (DiT) denoises in latent space, conditioned on T5 text and mel-spectrogram audio features via cross-attention.
  • Generates full-body SMPL-X motion (body, fingers, locomotion) at scale on the BEAT2, Embody3D and DnD datasets.
  • Research questions: making characters spatially and socially aware, preventing collisions while keeping positions and orientations right for mutual gaze, encoding multi-participant speech cheaply, and scaling to any number of participants.

Monocular Camera MoCap System
Retrocausal, Inc.  |  Redmond, WA  ·  Nov 2020 – Mar 2024

Markerless 3D motion capture from ordinary phone cameras for ergonomic safety analytics on the factory floor.

  • Built the MoCap product with a single colleague, winning a $150K/year contract with Siemens, and landed 3M as the company's first customer.
  • Experimented with monocular and multi-view approaches to extract 3D joint locations of workers; tackled monocular ambiguity with limb-length constraints and temporal smoothing.
